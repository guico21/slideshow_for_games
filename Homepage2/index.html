Good questions

FAQs

Tap any question to open it, or use the buttons to expand or collapse everything.

How do I confirm my spot?
Message the organiser and send your £35 entry before 29 November, 19:00. Your place is only locked once the payment lands in the night fund.
What does the £35 cover?
Food, drinks, game materials and prizes for the night. Any leftover fund rolls into the next edition.
Can I come if I don't know anyone?
Absolutely. The rounds are designed to mix teams. Come solo, leave with a new rivalry.
Is there a dress code?
Festive casual. Christmas jumpers are encouraged but not mandatory. Comfortable shoes are a good idea.
What happens if I can't make it?
Tell the organiser as early as possible. Entry fees are non-refundable once the fund has been spent, but you may be able to transfer your spot.
Where exactly at London Bridge?
We meet at the main London Bridge station entrance on the riverside side. Use “Open directions” at the top for a turn-by-turn map from wherever you are.
