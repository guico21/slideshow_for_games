The fine print

  • As in previous years, the games are for ages 10 and up and are mainly physical and team-based. Teams are created randomly for each game.
  • Games award scores to teams based on performance. Each game has a different average winning score.
  • During the evening there will be unforeseen events and opportunities that increase — or decrease — players' scores.
  • Cash prizes for the players finishing in 1st and 2nd place. Depending on the number of players, there may also be 3rd and 4th place prizes.
  • Given the interest from currently 20 people, this year we have decided to rent a venue. If any player has a venue (or room) available to accommodate everyone, please write to me privately.
  • The evening is entirely self-funded — all players contribute a share to cover prizes, food, drinks, tablecloths, venue rental and miscellaneous costs. This year the per-person fee is €22.
  • Any money remaining — not part of the prize pool — is returned to players in equal parts on the evening itself or via bank transfer.
  • The participation fee to confirm your presence must be paid via bank transfer by Saturday, 28 November 2026.

Good questions

FAQs

Tap any question to open it, or use the buttons to expand or collapse everything.

The per-player fee was lower in previous years. Why is it higher this year?
Given the higher number of interested people compared to previous years, the Cocola house will not be available to host us all. This requires an investment for an external venue, currently quantified in each player's fee.
Why do I have to pay the fee a month in advance?
Knowing the number of players in advance is essential for organising logistics — venue, game types, prizes and so on.
What happens if I get sick and can't play?
The player's fee will be returned minus the cost of the external venue rental.
What does it mean that any surplus money will be returned?
Assuming the total cost of the evening is €200 (including prizes for the winning players and the venue), and the total fees initially paid by the players were €400, the remaining €200 will be divided equally among all players and returned.
What if we have fewer than 20 people? Say, 10?
In that case, we will evaluate the hospitality of one of the players' homes, avoiding the rental of an external venue.
If we go to one of the players' homes, we don't pay for the venue. Can we set a lower fee?
In that case, there will surely be a higher percentage of money returned. The fee does not change (see questions 1 and 4).
If the number of players increases, the cost becomes lower per person, right?
Exactly. The more players there are, the more the costs are amortized (see question 2).
What is planned for food?
Take-out pizzas will be considered. The tastes will be mixed, as in previous years. Desserts are included for the end of the evening.
What is planned for drinks?
Mixed drinks, alcoholic and non-alcoholic. Water is included.
But the pizzerias are closed on the 27th. They were open in previous years.
If they are closed, other options will be considered. If there is nothing available, the players will prepare something to bring.
